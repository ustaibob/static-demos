Shortage Chain Contracts

Carboplatin, a core chemotherapy drug, is on national shortage. At a regional health system, a newly diagnosed patient now waits about five weeks from prescription to first infusion while pharmacists spend 10+ hours a week chasing supply. Your team designs the information contracts that keep six links coordinated. Every handoff below needs a direction, a channel, and a payload the receiver can act on.

  1. 1Set who sends (→ ← ⇄)
  2. 2Drag in a channel (protocol)
  3. 3Drag in the fields (schema)
  4. 4Define one field (semantics)
  5. 5Run the week, then add an inject
